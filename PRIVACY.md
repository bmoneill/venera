# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 18, 2026**

Venera ("Venera," "the Service," "we," "us," or "our") is a web application for
planning stargazing and astrophotography sessions using astronomical, weather,
and location data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Venera processes, why it is
processed, how long it is retained, and the rights available to users under
applicable privacy laws, including the European Union General Data Protection
Regulation ("GDPR") and the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA"), as amended
by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CPRA").

## 2. Information Venera Processes

Venera is designed to collect and retain as little personal information as
reasonably possible.

### Location Information

Some Venera features require a location in order to calculate astronomical
visibility, provide weather information, or recommend observing conditions.

You may provide a location through information such as:

- a municipality or place name; or
- latitude and longitude coordinates

**Venera does not retain or log the location information you provide for these purposes.**

Location information is processed only as necessary to perform the requested
calculation or provide the requested information. It is not associated with a
user account or maintained as a history of locations you have searched.

If device location services are used, your browser or operating system may
separately request your permission before providing location information to Venera.

### IP Addresses

When you access Venera, the server or hosting infrastructure may record your
**IP address** in server access or security logs.

IP addresses are used by Venera only for purposes such as:

- operating and securing the Service;
- detecting abuse, attacks, or unauthorized activity;
- diagnosing technical problems; and
- maintaining server reliability.

Venera does not use IP addresses to create user profiles, track users across
websites, serve targeted advertising, or intentionally determine or retain
users' physical locations.

Server access logs containing IP addresses are retained for
**no more than 30 days**, except where a particular record must be retained
longer to investigate a security incident, abuse, or legal obligation.

### Communications

If you voluntarily contact Venera by email or another provided communication
method, we may receive your email address and any information you include in
your message.

That information is used only to respond to the communication, address a
reported problem, or otherwise handle the reason you contacted us.

## 3. No User Accounts

Venera does not currently require user accounts and does not routinely collect

If account functionality is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will
be updated before or when such information begins to be collected.

## 4. Cookies and Tracking

Venera does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies.

Venera does not use personal information for behavioral or targeted advertising.

If analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies are introduced in the
future, this Privacy Policy and, where required, the Service's consent
mechanisms will be updated before those technologies are enabled.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Personal data processed by Venera is used only as reasonably necessary to:

- provide requested Venera functionality;
- calculate astronomical and observing information;
- obtain relevant weather information;
- operate and maintain the Service;
- protect Venera and its infrastructure against abuse or security threats;
- diagnose errors and technical problems;
- comply with applicable legal obligations; and
- respond to communications from users.

Venera does not use personal data for automated decisions that produce legal or
similarly significant effects.

## 6. Third-Party Services and Data Sources

Venera uses external services and datasets to provide its functionality.

### Open-Meteo

Venera uses Open-Meteo to obtain weather information.

When weather information is requested for a location, geographic coordinates
associated with the requested location may be transmitted to Open-Meteo as part
of the weather-data request.

Venera does not intentionally transmit your identity or your Venera access-log
IP address to Open-Meteo as part of that request.

Open-Meteo operates independently and may process requests according to its own
privacy practices.

### GeoNames

Venera uses GeoNames geographic data for municipality and location lookup.

Venera uses a locally imported copy of the relevant GeoNames dataset. User
searches therefore do not need to be sent to GeoNames merely to perform
municipality lookup.

### NASA JPL Ephemerides

Venera uses astronomical ephemeris data originating from NASA's Jet Propulsion
Laboratory for celestial calculations.

Venera uses a locally imported copy of the relevant JPL dataset. No user information
is sent to NASA.

### Hosting and Infrastructure Providers

The hosting, networking, or infrastructure providers used to operate Venera may
necessarily process technical connection information, including IP addresses, in
order to deliver requests to the Service and maintain their infrastructure.

Such providers may process information under their own legal obligations and
contractual terms.

## 7. Legal Bases for Processing Under the GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, Venera processes personal data under one or more of the
following legal bases:

### Legitimate Interests

IP addresses and related security information may be processed where necessary
for Venera's legitimate interests in operating, securing, maintaining, and
protecting the Service.

These interests are balanced against the privacy rights and interests of users,
and Venera limits the information retained for these purposes.

### Providing Requested Functionality

Location information supplied by a user is processed only as necessary to
provide the astronomical, weather, or observing functionality requested by that
user.

Where applicable law requires consent for access to device location information,
Venera will rely on the user's affirmative permission before obtaining that information.

### Legal Obligations

Information may be processed or retained when reasonably necessary to comply
with an applicable legal obligation.

## 8. Data Retention

Venera follows a data-minimization approach.

- **Location information:** processed transiently and not retained in Venera
  logs or databases.
- **IP access logs:** ordinarily deleted within 30 days.
- **Security-related records:** may be retained longer where reasonably
  necessary to investigate or document abuse or a security incident.
- **Email or other communications:** retained only for as long as reasonably
  necessary to respond to the communication, maintain relevant records, or
  satisfy applicable legal requirements.

When personal data is no longer reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose,
it is deleted or anonymized where practical.

## 11. California Privacy Rights

This section applies to California residents to the extent the CCPA or other
applicable California privacy law applies to Venera.

### Categories of Personal Information

During the preceding 12 months, Venera may have processed the following
categories of personal information:

- IP address.
- Source: automatically generated when a user connects to the Service.
- Purpose: operation, security, abuse prevention, and diagnostics.
- Sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising: **No**.

**Geolocation Information**

- Source: provided directly by the user or, with permission, by the user's device.
- Purpose: astronomical calculations, weather information, and observing recommendations.

**Venera does not sell personal information.**

personal information.

Where Venera processes precise location information, it is used only to provide
functionality specifically requested by the user and is not retained by Venera
or used to build a user profile.

Venera does not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that
require offering a separate right to limit such processing.
